About Me

I am an M.Sc. student in Digital Financial Technology at the National University of Singapore (School of Computing) and a research intern at the University of Notre Dame, working with Zheyuan Zhang and Prof. Yanfang (Fanny) Ye.

I work on the reliability and evaluation of large language models, agents, and multimodal models, and I have hands-on experience building efficient model architectures and inference systems. My work so far falls into two threads:

  • Reliability and evaluation: how compositional cues such as role labels and prompts shift vision-language models' judgments (preprint); how the point where a benchmark draws its “post-call” boundary changes labels and reported errors (ICAIF'26).
  • Efficient architectures and inference: depth-axis state space duality for transformers (COLM'26); inference-only acceleration for released vision-language-action checkpoints, without retraining (WWW'26 Demo).

I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

    When Does “Post-Call” Begin? An Availability Contract for Earnings-Call NLP Benchmarks

    Xiaoyu Ma, Kelvin J.L. Koa, Yuncong Liu, Ke-Wei Huang

  • WWW '26 Demo

    BLURR: A Boosted Low-Resource Inference for Vision-Language-Action Model

    Xiaoyu Ma*, Zhengqing Yuan*, Zheyuan Zhang, Kaiwen Shi, Lichao Sun, Yanfang Ye

    The Web Conference 2026 (WWW '26), Demo Track · DOI · Code

    An inference-only stack (BF16 execution, graph compilation, prefix KV caching, fewer decoding steps) that lowers per-step latency of released VLA checkpoints without retraining. My role: led the project end to end, from system design and implementation to experiments and the demo.

  • TII '26

    PCK-RL: A Unified Framework for Vision-Language Model and Reinforcement Learning-Based Robotic Manipulation

    Qianxi Li, Hongze Fu, Zhenyu Li, Yong Song, Chengjin Zhang, Xiaoyu Ma, Bao Pang

    IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, 22(8):7472–7483, 2026 · DOI

    A VLM splits a long-horizon task into keypoint-level subtasks, a locator grounds each keypoint in 3D, and an RL policy executes it. My role, over about ten months as a research assistant: proposed the PCA step of the keypoint locator (adapted from methods I knew from finance; it lets a 2D-only VLM choose keypoints on 3D objects), implemented parts of the core code, and took part in most experiments, from RL training and ablations in ManiSkill3 to real-robot trials on a UR5 arm.
