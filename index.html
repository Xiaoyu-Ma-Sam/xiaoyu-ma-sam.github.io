About

I am an M.Sc. student in Digital Financial Technology at the National University of Singapore (School of Computing) and a remote research intern at the University of Notre Dame, advised by Prof. Yanfang (Fanny) Ye and working closely with Zheyuan Zhang.

I study the reliability and evaluation of large language models, agents, and multimodal models: when a model’s output, or a benchmark’s score, can be trusted. I also build efficient model architectures and inference systems. My work so far follows two threads:

  • Reliability and evaluation. Do vision-language models judge what they see, or the cues in how a question is framed? (preprint) When does a benchmark’s timing quietly change its results? (ACM ICAIF ’26)
  • Efficient architectures and inference. How can depth-wise mixing in transformers become more expressive while staying efficient? (COLM ’26) Can released vision-language-action models run faster without retraining? (WWW ’26 Demo)

I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.
